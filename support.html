Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Email naorcho@gmail.com. We read every message and usually answer within a few days.

It helps to mention your phone model and what happened.

How do I play?

Move left and right under the falling balls and tap to jump. Touch a ball with your head to send it back up. Heading it right in the centre of your head while you are still rising gives a Perfect or MEGA header and the most points.

How do combos work?

Every header in a row adds to your combo, and Perfect and MEGA headers count double. At 3, 6, 10 and 15 your points are multiplied by x2, x3, x5 and x10. Missing a ball or getting hit resets the combo.

What should I avoid?

Boots, bottles and red cards cost a life. A yellow card books you, and a second yellow becomes a red card. Lose all three lives and the round ends early.

The tilt controls feel wrong

Open Settings from the menu. You can switch to touch controls, or tune tilt sensitivity, dead zone and response. Hold the phone the way you like to play and tap SET LEVEL to make that angle neutral.

Does the game need internet or collect my data?

No. Head Rush works offline and does not collect personal data. See the privacy policy.
