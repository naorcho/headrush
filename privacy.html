Privacy Policy

Head Rush: Football Headers · Effective 11 October 2026

Head Rush is made by Naor Melamed ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Head Rush on iPhone, iPad or Android. The short version: we do not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Head Rush has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not connect to the internet and does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your settings (such as control type, sound and vibration) and your best score in a file on your own device so they are there next time you play. This file never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Motion sensor

If you choose tilt controls, the game reads your device's motion sensor while you play to move the player. These readings are used instantly to steer and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Head Rush is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Parents with questions can contact us at the address below.

App stores

Head Rush is downloaded from Apple's App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about downloads, purchases or crashes under their own privacy policies, and depending on your device settings may share anonymous crash reports with developers. We do not receive anything that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Head Rush adds features that use data, such as online leaderboards, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
