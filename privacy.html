Privacy Policy

Head Rush: Football Headers · Effective 11 November 2026

Head Rush is made by Naor Melamed ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Head Rush on iPhone, iPad or Android. The short version: we do not collect, store or share any personal information. The optional leaderboard runs on Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, which Apple and Google operate.

Information we collect

None. Head Rush has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run any servers, and the game sends no information to us.

Leaderboard (Game Center and Google Play Games)

Head Rush has an optional "Best score" leaderboard. If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the game uses that existing account and sends the score of each finished round to the leaderboard, where other players can see it next to your gamer name. The Google Play Games software in the game also sends its own diagnostic data to Google.

Apple and Google handle this data under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google); we do not receive it. You can play without signing in: the game works the same, only the leaderboard is hidden. You choose your gamer name and who can see your profile, and you can delete your data, in your device's Game Center settings or in your Play Games profile.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your settings (such as control type, sound and vibration) and your best score in a file on your own device so they are there next time you play. This file never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Motion sensor

If you choose tilt controls, the game reads your device's motion sensor while you play to move the player. These readings are used instantly to steer and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Head Rush is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Whether a child can use Game Center or Google Play Games is controlled by Apple's and Google's family settings (Screen Time, Family Link). Parents with questions can contact us at the address below.

App stores

Head Rush is downloaded from Apple's App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about downloads, purchases or crashes under their own privacy policies, and depending on your device settings may share anonymous crash reports with developers. We do not receive anything that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Head Rush adds other features that use data, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
