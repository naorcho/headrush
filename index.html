A football game you play with your head

Balls rain down from the sky at every angle. Run under them, time your jump and head them back up. A clean header scores; a perfect one scores big. Keep the streak going and your combo multiplier climbs all the way to x10. You have 90 seconds.

Timing is everythingGood, Great, Perfect and MEGA headers. Hit the ball dead centre while rising for the big ones.
Special ballsGolden, fire, heavy and bonus balls, each with its own trick.
Watch outDodge flying boots, bottles and cards. Two yellows make a red!
Your controlsTilt your phone, follow your finger, or use a drag stick.
